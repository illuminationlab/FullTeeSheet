AI Tools

Your course never misses a call or a question again.

This is where FullTeeSheet earns its name. Two AI teammates cover the two places rounds leak out: the phone and the website.

Voice AI

The 24/7 receptionist on your phone.

Golf's phone problem is real: millions of hours a year, most of it the same questions about rates and open times, and rounds walk out the door every time a call goes to voicemail. Voice AI answers — greeting golfers naturally with real speech recognition, checking the live tee sheet, and taking action right on the call.

  • Answers from a knowledge base you control — green fees, twilight rates, cart policy, dress code, hours, range and lesson info, directions.
  • Checks the live tee sheet and books, reschedules, or cancels a tee time, suggesting open slots on the spot.
  • Creates contacts and outing opportunities in the CRM and triggers follow-up mid-call — a confirmation text, a deposit link, a note to the pro shop.
  • Transfers to a live person when a call needs one — a big outing, a complaint, a VIP member.
  • 200+ voices, your tone and pace, on your existing number — with a call summary and sentiment score for every call.

Conversation AI

Instant answers on every channel.

Conversation AI lives on your website chat widget and inside every channel — SMS, email, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, live chat — all in one inbox. It works your leads 24/7 with natural, on-brand replies.

  • Answers any question, pulling from a knowledge base you can edit any time — plus your documents, FAQs, and website via web crawler.
  • Qualifies the lead through normal conversation — whether it's a tee time, an outing, a league, or a membership.
  • Books right inside the chat, and escalates to you the moment a golfer wants a human.
  • Suggestive or fully automated — drafts a reply for staff to approve, or handles it end to end.
