Why We Exist

The right course. The wrong tools.

We kept watching golf courses get handed the wrong tools. Generic CRMs built for dentists and gyms with the word “golf” pasted on top. Enterprise club platforms priced for resorts, sold to munis that couldn’t carry them. Barter deals that traded away tee times and pricing control for “free” software. And a stack of five disconnected apps that never talked to each other.

Meanwhile the actual problems went unsolved — the phone nobody could answer on a busy Saturday, the cancellations that vanished, the outing lead that went cold over the weekend, the reviews that never got asked for.

We built FullTeeSheet to fix that specific gap.

This Is Our Whole World

Built for golf courses. Not restaurants. Not salons. Golf courses.

We don’t build for restaurants, salons, or clinics. We build for golf courses — daily-fee and public, municipals, semi-private and member clubs, resorts, and the operators just getting started. That focus is the difference between software that speaks tee sheets, green fees, outings, and no-shows, and software that makes you translate your business into someone else’s template.

Our Mission

Help golf courses grow affordably, with tools built for how they actually run — so a full tee sheet doesn’t depend on hiring another person you can’t find or afford.

What We Believe

Four principles that shape every decision.

The outcome matters more than the feature list.

Nobody wants more software. They want a full tee sheet and a phone that gets answered. We sell that outcome, and we only turn on the tools that get you there.

Modular beats monolithic.

You shouldn’t have to rip out what works to fix what doesn’t. Keep your tee sheet and POS. Add the pieces you’re missing. That’s how it should have always worked.

Your inventory and your pricing stay yours.

No barter, no giving away tee times to move them. You keep control of what your rounds are worth.

Transparent, not locked in.

One base — $99/month forever for the first 270 courses — plus what you use, and no contract built to trap you. We’d rather earn the renewal every month.
